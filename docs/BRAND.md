# SignalDesk — brand & product introduction

> For the website developer and logo designer.

## What SignalDesk is

SignalDesk is a **crypto & forex trading-signal app**. A strategy fires, and the user
gets a clean, real-time alert: what to trade, which direction, entry, stop-loss and
take-profit levels.

It is **guidance only** — SignalDesk never executes trades and is not a broker.

**One-line pitch:** *Trading signals, delivered fast — to your phone, in real time.*

## How it works (for context)

```
TradingView alert  →  backend  →  push notification  →  the app
```

Users open the app and see a live feed of open signals, each with entry, stop-loss and
take-profit targets, plus a closed-history of past results.

## Features (for the website)

- **Live signal feed** — newest signals first, real-time.
- **Signal details** — entry, stop-loss, all take-profit levels, direction, and change history.
- **Closed history** — past signals with result (TP / SL) and P/L %.
- **Position-size calculator** — crypto (units) and forex (lots).
- **Instant push alerts** — every signal arrives as a notification.
- **Two access tiers** — **Member** (regular) and **Partner** (full access to every signal).
- **Account** — email sign-in, password reset, account deletion.
- **Staff panel** — publish/edit/close signals, manage users.

## Brand feel

- **Dark, technical, precise** — like a professional trading terminal.
- **Calm, not flashy** — the data is the hero, the color is restrained.
- **Trustworthy and modern** — fintech, not gambling.

## Typography

- **Font:** Vazirmatn (primary), with system sans fallback.
- **Feel:** geometric, clean, modern — fits both Latin and Persian text.

## Logo brief

- **Concept:** a signal or pulse mark (a waveform, a rising arrow, or a "desk/terminal" motif).
- **Color:** sky blue (`#0ea5e9`) on a deep-navy background (`#0a0e17`), or sky blue alone.
- **Style:** minimal, geometric, sharp — nothing cartoony or overly literal.
- **Should read well** at app-icon size (small) and as a wordmark next to "SignalDesk".

The mark should feel like **"live signal"** — think pulse, wave, or an upward tick — not a
candlestick-heavy stock-chart cliché.
